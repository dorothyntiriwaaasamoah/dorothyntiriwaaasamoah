# Hi, I'm Dorothy Ntiriwaa Asamoah

**Marketing Strategist | Business Educator | Content Entrepreneur | Founder of Asantibiz**

My work sits at the intersection of **marketing strategy, business growth, content, data, and education.**

Through my professional brand, **KAN (Kaakyire Akosua Ntiriwaa)**, I explore how businesses can make better marketing decisions moving beyond simply being visible to building strategies that connect marketing activities to meaningful business outcomes.

I'm particularly interested in the questions businesses often overlook:

- Who exactly are we trying to reach?
- What does our customer actually need?
- Why should they choose us?
- Which marketing activities are working?
- How do we turn what we learn into better decisions?

## My Professional Journey

My perspective is shaped by both practice and research.

I am the founder of **Asantibiz**, a marketing and business growth agency built around four pillars:

**Build. Market. Grow. Educate.**

Through Asantibiz, I focus on helping businesses develop stronger foundations, market strategically, pursue sustainable growth, and build the knowledge needed to make informed business decisions.

### Dollie Spice
I also built **Dollie Spice**, a food content brand, where I have gained hands-on experience in:

- Audience building and community engagement
- Content strategy and digital marketing
- Product development
- Brand partnerships and collaborations
- Consumer engagement and brand positioning

These experiences have taught me that marketing looks very different when you are responsible for the actual business outcomes behind it.
